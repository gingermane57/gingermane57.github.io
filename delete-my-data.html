Data Deletion Request

GINGER MANE respects your privacy and your right to request the deletion of account-related data associated with our games.

If you would like to request the deletion of your account-related data, please send an email to:

gingermane57@gmail.com

Subject:
Data Deletion Request

Please include

  • The name of the game (for example: ROTRIS, MemoryPath or Sudoker).
  • Your Player ID, if available.
  • The device used to play the game (if relevant).

What happens next?

After receiving your request, GINGER MANE will review it and delete account-related data associated with the game where technically possible. This may include anonymous authentication identifiers and leaderboard-related data.

Data stored only on your device, such as local settings, currencies, missions or unlocked content, can be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its local app data.

Some information may be retained when required by applicable law, security requirements or fraud-prevention obligations.

Most requests are processed within 30 days.
